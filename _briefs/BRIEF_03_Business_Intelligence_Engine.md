### **SECTION 4: HOW IT WORKS (EXAMPLE)**

**Headline (H2):**
```
Intelligence in Action: How It Powers Everything
```

**Scenario:**

```
YOUR BUSINESS CONTEXT:
• Industry: Financial advisory (RIA)
• Service: Retirement planning for high-net-worth individuals
• Differentiator: Fee-only, fiduciary approach
• Compliance: SEC Marketing Rule 206(4)-1

WHAT THE INTELLIGENCE ENGINE DOES:

Market Intelligence:
→ Identifies that "fiduciary" is a high-intent keyword in your market
→ Discovers competitors emphasizing "free consultations"
→ Finds gap: Few advisors educate on fee structures transparently

Business Context:
→ Extracts your brand voice from website (professional, educational)
→ Maps your service (retirement planning, not day trading)
→ Identifies your differentiator (fee-only = transparency)

Audience Intelligence:
→ Profiles ideal client (45-65, $2M+ net worth, pre-retirement)
→ Tracks intent signals (pricing page visits, fee structure downloads)
→ Segments by journey stage (awareness vs. decision)

AI Strategy:
→ Creates content themes: "Understanding Fiduciary Duty," "Fee-Only vs. Commission"
→ Suggests LinkedIn + targeted email (where HNW professionals engage)
→ Personalizes messaging by segment
→ Ensures SEC compliance in all output

RESULT:
Every campaign reflects YOUR positioning, targets YOUR ideal client, 
and speaks to what actually matters in YOUR market.

Not generic. Intelligent.
```
