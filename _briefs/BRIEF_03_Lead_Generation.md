#### **Component 2: Lead Scoring & Segmentation**

**Headline (H3):** Lead Scoring & Segmentation

**What It Is:**
```
Automatic scoring based on behavior, demographics, and engagement. Leads are classified as hot, warm, or cold—so your sales team focuses on people ready to buy.
```

**Scoring Model (visual breakdown):**

**Behavioral Scoring:**
- Visited pricing page: +20 points
- Downloaded guide: +10 points
- Opened email: +5 points
- Clicked link in email: +10 points
- Visited blog post: +3 points
- 3+ page visits in one session: +15 points

**Demographic Scoring:**
- Title includes "Owner" or "Partner": +15 points
- Company size matches ICP: +10 points
- Industry matches target: +10 points

**Engagement Scoring:**
- Active in last 7 days: +10 points
- Active in last 30 days: +5 points
- Inactive 60+ days: -10 points

**Segmentation:**
- 50+ points = 🔥 Hot Lead (sales alert)
- 25-49 points = 🟡 Warm Lead (nurture sequence)
- 0-24 points = 🔵 Cold Lead (awareness content)

**The Result:**
```
Your sales team gets real-time alerts when leads are hot. Marketing nurtures warm leads automatically. No one falls through cracks.
```

---

#### **Component 3: Lead Attribution & Tracking**

**Headline (H3):** Multi-Touch Attribution & Tracking

**What It Is:**
```
Complete journey tracking from first touch to closed deal. Know exactly which campaigns generate leads and which touchpoints influence conversions.
```

**Attribution Models:**

**First-Touch Attribution**
```
Which campaign generated the lead originally?
Example: Lead came from LinkedIn ad, converted after 5 touchpoints
Credit: LinkedIn ad gets 100% attribution for acquisition
```

**Last-Touch Attribution**
```
Which campaign drove the final conversion?
Example: Lead originally from LinkedIn, converted after pricing page email
Credit: Email gets 100% attribution for conversion
```

**Multi-Touch Attribution (Recommended)**
```
Every touchpoint gets credit proportionally
Example: LinkedIn ad (40%) + Blog post (20%) + Email 1 (15%) + Email 2 (15%) + Pricing page (10%)
Result: See full journey, optimize entire funnel
```

**What Gets Tracked:**
- Ad clicks (paid social, Google Ads)
- Email opens and clicks
- Website visits and page views
- Form submissions
- Content downloads
- Social media engagement
- Phone calls (with call tracking)
- CRM activities (meetings scheduled, proposals sent)

**The Result:**
```
See exactly which campaigns generate revenue. Shift budget to what works. Stop wasting money on campaigns that don't convert.
```
